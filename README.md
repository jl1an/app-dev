## About the Movie
**F1** is a racing movie about a veteran driver who returns to Formula 1 to help a struggling team and mentor a young rookie.
It's *fast*, *loud*, and really fun to watch.

## Why I Like It
- I am a Formula 1 fan
- I also drive a car myself
- The racing scenes feel real
- The story about teamwork and second chances
- The movie soundtrack is really great, and one of my favorite artists has a song on it.
